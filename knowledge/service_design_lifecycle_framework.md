# S5 | Design Lifecycle Framework (Service Design in Context)

> **Context:** Service design is a subpart of a larger, integrated design and technology delivery lifecycle. This framework provides standard industry terminology, capability mapping, and operational practices across five distinct phases. Use this taxonomy to position portfolio case studies, map development pipelines, and align with enterprise service design / consulting benchmarks.

---

## The 5 Design Lifecycle Phases

```
[ 1. Research & Insight ] ──> [ 2. Service Design ] ──> [ 3. Business Design ] ──> [ 4. Product & Experience Design ] ──> [ 5. Platform Design ] ──> [ Delivery / Live ]
```

---

### Phase 1: Research & Insight
*Focus: Deep discovery, user behavior, performance benchmarking, and evaluative research.*

* **Lab-based UX research**
* **Lab set-up and capability build**
* **UX analysis and benchmarking**
* **Accessibility testing**
* **Information architecture testing**
* **Survey and VoC (Voice of Customer) programme design**
* **Digital analytics set-up, tag management and reporting / analysis**
* **Measurement framework design**
* **Social listening**
* **A/B and multivariate testing**
* **Concept experiments (smoke testing)**

---

### Phase 2: Service Design
*Focus: Holistic end-to-end ecosystems connecting customer experience to frontline and back-office staff operations.*

* **Ethnographic research scoping, conducting and analysis**
* **Persona, journey and blueprint design – for both customer and employee experience**
* **Ideation and concept creation**
* **Customer and employee experience visioning**
* **Low-fi experience prototyping – combining physical and digital**
* **Service design measurement and analytics**
* **Customer experience led transformation**
* **Channel strategy**

---

### Phase 3: Business Design
*Focus: Commercial viability, operating models, proposition testing, and strategic alignment.*

* **Analyse market and competitive dynamics**
* **Business strategy and business model design**
* **Innovation strategy and management**
* **Proposition design and testing**
* **Experiment design and lean start-up**
* **Business modelling, business case and value analysis**
* **Brand and marketing strategy**
* **Customer experience strategy**
* **Product strategy and ownership**

---

### Phase 4: Product & Experience Design
*Focus: Interaction craft, visual hierarchy, UX writing, and design system scalability.*

* **Creative & Experience Direction**
* **Brand positioning**
* **Creative concepting**
* **Accessibility, usability & heuristics**
* **Information Architecture design**
* **UX & UI design**
* **Content Strategy**
* **UX writing**
* **Design prototyping**
* **Agile design delivery**
* **Design system creation / design at scale**
* **Graphic Design & Illustration**
* **Animation & Video Editing**

---

### Phase 5: Platform Design
*Focus: Technical enablement, scalable architecture, engineering execution, and emerging technologies.*

* **Product technology**
* **Solution architecture**
* **Software engineering**
* **Product management**
* **Emerging technologies:**
  * **Virtual Reality (VR)**
  * **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**
  * **Mobile**
  * **Blockchain**
  * **Cloud**
  * **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**
