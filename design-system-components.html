4. Long Description

Card with yellow/amber header, crisp white container, and typewriter/monospace typography for structured UX specifications.

Business Goal

After Brexit, the company needed to stay competitive as the client began exploring equivalent tunnel-management solutions within Sweden and the EU.

User Story:

Olof is a traffic officer he has to constantly zoom in and out of a geographic map–finding one icon and back in to another. When multiple devices along the same road section need updating at once, this process becomes slow, stressful, and error-prone. Olof needs a schematic view that lets him understand and control equipment logically, not geographically
