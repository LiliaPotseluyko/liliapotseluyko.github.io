A mobile-friendly interface is possible and optimal for field operations.

## Sub-Page 5: Iterative Development

### [ID: sp5-title] — Sub-Page 5 Title

Iterative Development

### [ID: sp5-findings-intro] — Research Findings Intro

In the process of prototype testing and agile development, I was able to test workflow assumptions, identify new feature requests, define MVP product boundaries, and conduct benchmarking:

### [ID: sp5-finding1-desc] — Finding 1: Testing Workflow Assumptions

Tested operational highways workflows with users, validating where automation accelerates decisions and where human verification remains essential.

### [ID: sp5-finding2-desc] — Finding 2: Historical Defect Visualization

Uncovered a critical user need to inspect historical defect progression across road sections, distinguishing recurring structural failures from routine cosmetic wear.

### [ID: sp5-finding3-desc] — Finding 3: Invoice Monitoring System

Identified that field contractor adoption depended on commercial clarity, integrating transparent cost forecasting and invoice status tracking directly into repair handoffs.

### [ID: sp5-finding4-desc] — Finding 4: Defining MVP Product Boundaries

Eliminated enterprise feature bloat to focus the MVP strictly on high-leverage workflows for two core personas: Asset Managers and Regional Service Providers.

### [ID: sp5-finding5-desc] — Finding 5: Interaction Benchmarking

Leveraged proven interaction patterns from map navigation and enterprise logistics platforms, significantly reducing operator onboarding friction.
