Assumptions

Core design assumptions and hypotheses guiding our rapid prototyping trajectory:

01

Mobile-Friendly Interface • Progressive Complexity

Assumption: A mobile-friendly interface is possible and optimal for field operations.

Leveraging the UX framework of progressive complexity: we began with mobile-first constraints to prioritize core actions and facilitate fast prototyping, before expanding functionality outward to full desktop control centers.

02

Industry Readiness for Unified Automation

Assumption: The highways maintenance industry is ready for automated vehicle repairs.

Assuming that disparate multi-domain services—smart self-healing materials, autonomous robotic crack sealers, and predictive AI data science—can be unified into a single, cohesive product ecosystem.

Experience Learned • C4 Model

To resolve communication silos across robotics, smart materials, data science, and digital twin teams, I adopted the C4 Architecture Model to formalize system boundaries and standardize inputs and outputs before writing glue code:

1 • Context
Highways maintenance ecosystem, partner contractors & external APIs
2 • Container
Web app, PostGIS DB, Unreal Engine twin & autonomous repair platforms
3 • Component
Task Dispatcher, Triage Engine, GIS microservice & User Services
4 • Code
Defect classification algorithms, API schemas & state machine handlers
  • Explicit Contracts: Established concrete JSON input/output contracts between data science treatment models and spatial twins.
  • Silo Elimination: Enabled parallel multidisciplinary prototyping without integration bottlenecks or schema drift.
02 • Algorithmic Intelligence

Data Science Treatment Engine

Algorithmic mapping between detected highway defects, root cause, and automated maintenance treatment recommendations.

Inspect Full Res
1. Highway Defects Transverse, longitudinal & alligator cracking, ravelling, rutting, road marking degradation, and drainage failure.
2. Root Causes Heavy traffic axle loading, moisture penetration, aggregate design mix aging, thermal contraction, and soil subsidence.
3. Treatment Strategies Preventative sealing, lane speed reduction, asphalt mix redesign, isolation, and scheduled structural resurfacing.
  • Causal Reasoning: Moves beyond reactive visual recognition to infer underlying degradation physics and recommend root-cause repairs.
  • Decision Automation: Feeds qualified treatment recommendations directly into task order generation for human review or robotic dispatch.
03 • Target Architecture

Unified Conceptual Architecture

The final synthesized system architecture connecting autonomous vehicles, geospatial digital twins, and human operators.

Inspect Full Res
Module 1 • Data Acquisition Ingests raw road issue telemetry, sensor streams, and point clouds from survey vehicles and mobile probes.
Module 2 • Geospatial Digital Twin PostGIS spatial database and GIS microservice managing 3D road assets, defect coordinates, and queryable tiles.
Module 3 • Autonomous Module RoadGP triage core, Task Dispatcher, and AMP Orchestrator driving autonomous physical maintenance platforms.
Module 4 • Human Module Secure API Gateway, User Data Service, 3D Engine, and interactive web interface for engineer review and approvals.
  • Human-in-the-Loop: Bi-directional task orders ensure automated triage executes rapidly while reserving high-budget repairs for human assessor sign-off.

Findings

In the process of prototype testing and agile development, I was able to test workflow assumptions, identify new feature requests, define MVP product boundaries, and conduct benchmarking:

  • Testing Workflow Assumptions

    Tested operational highways workflows with users, validating where automation accelerates decisions and where human verification remains essential.

  • Historical Defect Visualization

    Uncovered a critical user need to inspect historical defect progression across road sections, distinguishing recurring structural failures from routine cosmetic wear.

  • Invoice Monitoring System

    Identified that field contractor adoption depended on commercial clarity, integrating transparent cost forecasting and invoice status tracking directly into repair handoffs.

  • Defining MVP Product Boundaries

    Eliminated enterprise feature bloat to focus the MVP strictly on high-leverage workflows for two core personas: Asset Managers and Regional Service Providers.

  • Interaction Benchmarking

    Leveraged proven interaction patterns from map navigation and enterprise logistics platforms.

Opportunity Solution Tree: Full Feature Specifications

User and stakeholder priority results across continuous discovery categories

Maintenance planning and prioritisation

  • Improve the ability to prioritise repairs based on safety, urgency, operational constraints, available resources, and proactive lifecycle planning.
  • Use academic research findings to develop deterioration predictions that support repair planning.
  • Optimise service provider repair routes and resource allocation by integrating Rui’s PhD research.
  • Improve communication between reactive and proactive maintenance teams through a shared interface, helping reduce the cost of expensive reactive maintenance.

Visualisation and repair delivery

  • Improve defect visualisation through maps, defect polygons, extracted imagery, and 360° images that provide location and surrounding-context information, road clousure arrangments
  • Provide transparent visualisation of repair history.
  • Integrate visualisation with job and invoicing systems to clarify repair tasks and support completion checks.
  • Collect information about failed repairs through the interface to improve future decision-making.

RoadGP-specific features

  • Integrate RoadGP with existing systems so repairs can be designed and planned using available resource information.
  • Provide clear data-input interfaces to improve RoadGP recommendations and decision support.
  • Enable users to request team opinions on decisions, helping to standardise decision-making and improve efficiency.
  • Automate defect clustering to create assessment segments for RoadGP treatment advice.
  • Connect RoadGP defect clusters at 15 m, National Highways network-model segments at 100 m, and repair job areas at 10 km through the interface.

RICE Score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort
Standardized 1–4 scale: Reach (1=Local • 4=National Network), Impact (1=Minor • 4=Transformative), Confidence (1=Low/Early • 4=High/Tested Evidence), Effort (1=Rapid Build • 4=Heavy Multi-Year R&D).

2×2 Feature Matrix: Feasibility vs Impact Analysis

Strategic continuous discovery mapping balancing technical feasibility against operational maintenance impact

Quick Wins (High Impact • High Feasibility) Priority 1

Immediate low-friction deliverables providing outsized clarity to inspectors and contractors.

  • Visualisation Clarity: Defect map polygons, extracted imagery, and 360° location context for instant road situation awareness.
  • RoadGP Clustering: Automated defect clustering to aggregate individual road defects into cohesive assessment segments (15 m).
  • Repair Prioritisation: Multi-factor ranking scoring repairs by urgency, safety criticality, and operational constraints.
  • ML Data Input Interfaces: Standardized UI forms to feed inspection parameters into RoadGP predictive engines.
Strategic Bets (High Impact • Lower Feasibility) Priority 2

Transformative algorithmic capabilities requiring advanced data science model training and cross-system integration.

  • Deterioration Prediction: Integrating PhD academic deterioration models to forecast pavement decay trajectories over time.
  • Cluster Treatment Advice: Context-aware algorithmic recommendation of optimal surface treatments per cluster.
  • Multi-Scale Network Bridging: Synchronizing 15 m defect clusters, 100 m NH network models, and 10 km maintenance contract parcels.
Fill-Ins (Lower Impact • High Feasibility) Priority 3

Straightforward interface conveniences that streamline communication without core architectural dependencies.

  • Repair History View: Auditable historical timeline of past road surface interventions and contractor notes.
  • Reactive & Proactive Shared View: Unified status view bridging emergency repair gangs and scheduled maintenance planners.
Deprioritised / Deferred (Lower Impact • Lower Feasibility) Deferred

High complexity integrations with peripheral enterprise systems that do not advance core maintenance decisions.

  • Invoicing Systems & Billing Sync: Automated accounting and commercial invoicing integration (high external IT friction, non-core).
  • Ad-Hoc Team Opinion Polling: Unstandardized consensus polling queues that slow down decisive automated recommendations.

MoSCoW Prioritisation: RoadGP MVP Scope Boundaries

Release classification defining baseline MVP commitments versus phased product horizons

MUST HAVE

Core MVP Non-Negotiables

Essential for platform launch. Without these components, the maintenance triage and RoadGP calculation pipeline cannot operate.

  • Cluster Treatment Advice: Context-aware algorithmic recommendation of optimal surface treatments per defect cluster.
  • Defect Map & 360° Context View: Interactive GIS defect polygons with surrounding environmental imagery for remote inspection.
  • Automated Defect Clustering: Clustering algorithm grouping spatial defects at 15 m intervals for actionable work packages.
  • Repair Prioritisation Engine: Algorithmic scoring ranking defects by safety hazard, depth, and traffic impact.

SHOULD HAVE

High-Value Fast-Follow

Important capabilities delivering high stakeholder value that can follow immediately once core data feeds stabilize.

  • Deterioration Curves Prediction: Academic modeling forecasting when non-critical cracks will escalate into potholes.
  • Repair History Audit Trail: Track past surface treatments to avoid recurring premature road failures.
  • Standardized ML Data Input Forms: Explicit parameters inputs allowing engineers to tweak predictive variables.

COULD HAVE

Phase 2 Enhancements

Desirable operational enhancements scoped for subsequent releases to enrich contractor delivery workflows.

  • Route & Resource Optimisation: Optimizing repair vehicle dispatch sequences and supply depot trips.
  • Team Decision Verification: Structured peer review and sign-off queues before treatment contract commitment.

WON'T HAVE

Out of MVP Scope

Explicitly excluded from the RoadGP software MVP to protect delivery focus and prevent boundary creep.

  • ERP & Invoicing Sync: Direct automated commercial billing and contractor payment reconciliation.
  • Direct Machine Control for AMP: Autonomous Maintenance Plant robotic guidance (hardware scope, Jerry's stream).
  • Heavy 3D Mesh Streaming: Real-time national volumetric point-cloud mesh streaming (Digital Twin scope).
