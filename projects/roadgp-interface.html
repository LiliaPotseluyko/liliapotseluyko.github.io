Goal & Hypothesis
Goal

To deliver an intuitive interface bridging emerging AI automation with legacy maintenance workflows.

Hypothesis

By enabling automated treatment recommendations, automating repair tasks and streamlining communication across stakeholders, we believe maintenance teams will make higher-quality repair decisions, reduce delays and information gaps between reactive and proactive maintenance, and deliver more effective interventions.

Storyboard & Core Assumptions

Foundation

Assumptions

Core design assumptions and hypotheses guiding our rapid prototyping trajectory:

01

Mobile-Friendly Interface • Progressive Complexity

Assumption: A mobile-friendly interface is possible and optimal for field operations.

Leveraging the UX framework of progressive complexity: we began with mobile-first constraints to prioritize core actions and facilitate fast prototyping in the field, before expanding functionality outward to full desktop control centers.

02

Industry Readiness for Unified Automation

Assumption: The highways maintenance industry is ready for automated vehicle repairs.

Assuming that disparate multi-domain services—smart self-healing materials, autonomous robotic crack sealers, and predictive AI data science—can be unified into a single, cohesive product ecosystem.
